Questions

Common questions.

What does LootPick do?

LootPick tracks video game prices across digital stores. You add a game to your watchlist and LootPick notifies you when the price drops to a good deal.

Is LootPick free?

Yes. LootPick is free to use with a watchlist limit. LootPick Premium removes the limit and unlocks the full free games feed.

What does Open Chord do?

Open Chord teaches guitar from scratch. It includes chord diagrams, a tuner, scales, modes, riffs, and ear training exercises, all in one app.

Is Open Chord free?

Yes. Open Chord is free with no subscription and no paywalls. Ads are coming in a future update to keep it that way.

When do the apps launch?

Open Chord is out now on Android and arrives on iOS at the end of August 2026. LootPick launches on Android on 31 August 2026, on iOS on 15 September 2026, and on web on 30 September 2026.
