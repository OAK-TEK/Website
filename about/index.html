About

A small studio, building things we actually want to use.

Who We Are

Independent app studio

OAK TEK is an independent app studio based in Dubai. We build tools for the hobbies we care about — and we use every one of them ourselves. If we wouldn't put it on our own phone, we don't ship it.

We started because we kept running into the same small problems — no good way to track game prices across platforms, no guitar app that actually teaches instead of just showing chord charts. So we built what we wanted.

We're a small team and we like it that way. It means every decision is deliberate, every feature earns its place, and we can move fast without compromising on quality. We'd rather ship two great apps than ten mediocre ones.

What We Build

Focused apps for real problems.

Price tracking

LootPick monitors game prices across stores and alerts you when it's time to buy. No manual checking, no missed deals.

Music learning

Open Chord teaches guitar from scratch — chords, tuning, scales, ear training. Built for people who want to play, not just watch.

What's next

We're always looking at the hobbies around us. When we find a problem worth solving, we build for it. Got an idea?
