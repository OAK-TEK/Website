About

We build what we use.

Who we are

Independent app studio.

OAK TEK is an independent app studio based in Dubai. We build tools for the hobbies we care about, and we use every one of them ourselves. If we wouldn't put it on our own phone, we don't ship it.

We started because we kept hitting the same road bumps. No good way to track game prices across stores. No guitar app that actually teaches instead of just showing chord charts. So we built what we wanted.

We're small and we like it that way. Every decision is deliberate, every feature earns its place, and we can move fast without cutting corners. We'd rather ship two apps we're proud of than ten we're not.

What we build

Apps for real problems.

Price tracking

LootPick watches game prices across stores and tells you when to buy. No manual checking, no missed deals.

Music learning

Open Chord teaches guitar from scratch. Chords, tuning, scales, ear training. Out now, free on Android.

What's next

We're always looking at the hobbies around us. When we find a problem worth solving, we build for it. Got an idea?
