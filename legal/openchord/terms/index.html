Play Safely

Playing guitar is a physical activity. Please take these points seriously:

  • Hearing. The App produces sound, including metronome clicks and note playback. Keep your device and any headphones at a moderate volume. Prolonged exposure to loud audio can cause permanent hearing damage.
  • Strain and injury. Stop and rest if you feel pain, numbness, tingling, or persistent discomfort in your hands, wrists, arms, neck, or back. Seek medical advice if symptoms continue. Do not push through pain to keep a practice streak alive.
  • Attention. Do not use the App while driving, cycling, or doing anything else that needs your attention.

You use the App at your own risk and are responsible for practising within your own physical limits.
