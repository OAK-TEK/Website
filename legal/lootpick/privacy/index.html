2. The short version

  • You can browse LootPick as a guest. Guest lists stay in your browser or on your device.
  • If you create an account we hold your email address, your username and what you add to LootPick: Picks (Wishlist), Loot (games you own), alerts, ratings, reviews, settings and, if you choose, Steam data.
  • Ratings and reviews are public. They appear with your username to anyone who opens the game page.
  • We never see your card number. Payments are handled by Google Play and the App Store (mobile app) and by Stripe through RevenueCat (website).
  • Free accounts see ads, except during the free Premium window (1 November to 31 December 2026), when nobody sees them. Google receives data for that purpose.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We send occasional product news emails to account holders. Each one has an unsubscribe link, and you can turn them off in Notifications (app) or Settings (website). Alert and account emails are not affected.
  • You can delete your account yourself, in the app or on the website.

3.2 Steam connection (optional)

If you connect Steam, we store your Steam ID and the Steam name, profile link or ID you typed to find your account. Using the Steam Web API, from our servers, we then read:

  • your owned games, with total hours played, hours played in the last two weeks, and the date you last played each game, which we import into your Loot and refresh when you sync again;
  • your public Steam wishlist, if you import it, which we add to your Picks (Wishlist);
  • for a game whose page you open, your achievements for that game (which are unlocked and when, plus global unlock rates). We store only the unlocked count and total against your copy of that game.

This works only for data your Steam privacy settings make public (profile, game details and Steam wishlist). We do not ask for your Steam password. We do not post, change or delete anything on your Steam account, and we do not read your friends list or chat. Steam does not tell us what you paid or when you bought a game, so imported games have no price until you add one. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw by disconnecting in Settings. Disconnecting clears the stored Steam ID and name and stops new reads. Games already imported stay in your Loot and Picks (Wishlist) until you delete them.

Push notifications (App only). If you allow them, OneSignal stores a push token, the device name and the platform so a message can reach your phone, and we keep the token linked to your account. We use it to tell you when a price you watch drops or reaches your target, when a tracked game is released, and when a free game appears, following the notification settings you choose in the App. The Website does not itself send push notifications. Alerts set up on the Website can be delivered by email, or by push to your linked App device if you choose that in Settings. Turn notifications off in the App or your device settings and the token stops being used. Uninstalling the App does not delete the token, but deleting your account removes it from our database. OneSignal keeps its own copy under its own retention period. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Crash and error reports (App only). We use Sentry to receive crash and error reports. We switch off Sentry's default collection of personal data and we do not attach your name, email address or account id to a report. This is not controlled by the analytics setting, because it is how we find out the App has broken. Sentry keeps reports under its own retention period.

Release reminders and your calendar (App only). If you turn on release date reminders and allow calendar access, the App adds an event to a calendar on your device for each game in your Picks (Wishlist) that has a future release date, and updates it if the date moves. We do not read the contents of your other calendar entries, and we do not send calendar data to our servers. If the calendar on your device is synced by your calendar provider, that provider handles the events under its own policy. You can turn reminders off in Notifications and remove the permission in your device settings.

3.4 Guest use

6. Game data that is not about you

Game titles, artwork, prices, release dates, scores and news come from IGDB, CheapShark, IsThereAnyDeal, OpenCritic, GamerPower, FreeToGame, Steam and public news feeds, requested by our servers or your browser without sending your account data. Game images are loaded from the providers' image servers (such as IGDB and Steam), which can see your IP address when your browser loads them. Games metadata is powered by IGDB.com.

We also keep a price history: once a day we store the lowest price we saw for each game, with the store and discount. It is about games, not about you, and it is not tied to any account.

7. Public information: ratings, reviews and community counts

When you rate a game or write a review, your username, your score, your review text, the date, and whether it is marked as containing spoilers are visible to people who open that game page. The Website shows usernames to signed-in visitors only, but the review data behind a game page can be requested by anyone, so treat your username as public. We do not show your email address, account identifier, or the contents of your Loot. Game pages also show anonymous totals: how many users have the game in Picks (Wishlist) or Loot and the average rating.

Reviews are limited to 400 characters and are checked automatically for profanity before they post. A rating and review are removed when you set a game back to "Not played", remove your last copy from your Loot, or delete your account. Do not include personal data about yourself or others in a review.

Reports and moderation. To report a review, use the Report link under it or email play@lootpick.quest. Each report reaches us with your username, the reason you chose and any note. When three different players report the same review it is hidden from everyone except its author while we look at it, and we email the author. When we decide, we email the author our decision and the reason, and we email each player who reported it the outcome. We do not tell the author who reported them. These emails go through Brevo (Section 5).

Blocking. If you block a player, their reviews are hidden from you. They are not told. You can undo it on the website in Settings, under Blocked users, or by emailing us.

8. Advertising

Free accounts may see ads. To serve and measure ads Google may collect device identifiers, cookies, approximate location from your IP address and device information. This is the one area where a third party uses your data for its own purposes.

8A. Website. Website ads are not running yet. Once we switch them on, ads will be served by Google AdSense on public pages only (not on sign-in, Picks (Wishlist), Loot, Settings, Profile or Premium pages). Nothing for advertising loads until you accept advertising cookies (Section 4A). Ad-Free and Premium subscribers see no ads and the AdSense script does not load for them.

8B. App. Ads are served by Google AdMob, to Free accounts only. Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, personalised advertising depends on your consent through Google's consent prompt in the App. On iPhone and iPad we also ask for your permission through Apple's tracking prompt before the advertising identifier is used for ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Ad-Free and Premium subscribers see no ads, and the advertising SDK does not start for them when the App opens. The same applies to every account during the free Premium window (1 November to 31 December 2026): no ads, no consent form and no tracking prompt appear while it lasts. If you upgrade while the App is open, ads stop at once and the SDK does not start again the next time you open it.

11. Deleting your account

  • Website: Settings, then Danger zone, then Delete account. You type DELETE and confirm with a one-time code sent to your email.
  • Mobile app: Profile, then Delete Account. You type DELETE and confirm with a code sent to your email.
  • By email: write to play@lootpick.quest from the address on the account. We respond within 30 days.

Deletion removes your Picks (Wishlist), Loot (including hours and achievement counts), alerts, ratings and reviews, reports you sent, blocks, email log, profile, Steam link and sign-in from our own application database and from Clerk. It cannot be undone. If Clerk deletes a user by any other route, an automated process removes the same data from our database. It does not automatically delete records held by third parties: your customer and transaction records at RevenueCat and Stripe (kept for tax, accounting and fraud purposes), your contact and email event log at Brevo, provider server logs, and backups, which clear on their own schedule. Those follow the providers' own retention periods. Email us if you want a provider asked to delete its copy sooner.

Subscriptions. If you have a website subscription, deleting your account cancels it and it remains active until the end of the paid period. A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play cannot be cancelled by us. Cancel it in that store or it will keep billing after your account is gone.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; receive it in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), including opting out of product news with the unsubscribe link in the email or the switch in Notifications or Settings; and not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. LootPick's Buy Now Score is informational and has no such effect.

How to use them. Much of this you can do yourself in Settings or the App. For anything else email play@lootpick.quest. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within 30 days, or the shorter period the law requires. If we refuse a request, tell us and we will explain why and what you can do next.

Complaints. You may complain to your local data protection regulator. In the UAE that is the UAE Data Office under the PDPL. Many other countries we serve, including Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Brazil, Türkiye, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, Egypt and Morocco, have their own privacy laws and regulators, and we honour the rights those laws give you in the same way.

California. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising cookies and identifiers may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. On the Website they are off unless you accept them, you can withdraw under Cookie settings in the footer, and we honour the Global Privacy Control signal (Section 4A). In the App you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

13. Children

LootPick is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We ask for a confirmation that you are 13 or older when you sign up, and we do not collect your date of birth. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it and the data behind it.

14. Security

We use encryption in transit, row-level security in our database so that account rows are readable only by their owner, secret keys kept server-side, and re-verification (an emailed code) for irreversible actions such as deleting an account. Unsubscribe links in our emails are signed, so a link works only for the account it was sent to. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

15. Automated processing

The Buy Now Score is calculated automatically from price, discount, ratings and your own list. It is informational only, does not produce legal effects, and you can ignore it.

16. Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. For significant changes, such as a new category of data, a new purpose, or a new advertising practice, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
