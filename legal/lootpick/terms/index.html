LootPick aggregates game information, store prices, discounts, price history and review scores, and produces a Buy Now Score to help you decide when to buy. It also lets you keep Picks (Wishlist), Loot (games you own, with prices, hours and ratings), price alerts, and view free-game giveaways.

  • Independent. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any publisher, developer, storefront or platform holder (including Valve, Microsoft, Sony, Nintendo, Epic Games, Google or Apple).
  • Information only. Prices, discounts, scores, release dates, giveaways and achievement data come from third parties and can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Always confirm the price on the store page before you pay.
  • Price history. We record the lowest price we see for a game each day, starting 21 October 2026, so the price history chart (a Premium feature) fills up over time and appears once a game has 30 days of history. It shows what we saw. It can have gaps and does not predict what a price will do.
  • Adult games. LootPick keeps games it identifies as sexually explicit out of search, browse and similar-game lists. The filter uses the game database's tags and some title keywords, so it can miss a game or hide one by mistake. A game already in your Picks (Wishlist) or Loot, or opened from a link, still works.
  • Buy Now Score. It is our own calculation, an opinion expressed as a number. It is not advice, a valuation, or a prediction that a price will or will not fall. What you buy is your decision.
  • Currency. Where you choose a display currency other than the source currency, converted amounts use an indicative fixed rate. They are approximations, not quotes.
  • Your records. Purchases, prices paid, hours and statuses in Loot are entered by you (or imported without prices from Steam). We do not verify them, and totals and savings figures are only as accurate as your inputs. Treat them as a personal tracker, not a financial record.
  • Alerts are best effort. We do not guarantee that an alert will fire, arrive in time, or that a deal will still be live. Push notifications are sent by the App and only if you allow them on your device. On the Website, alerts can be delivered by email, and if your account has a linked App device you can choose push delivery in Settings.
